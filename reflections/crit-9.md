# Crit 9 reflection

The breakthrough was finding this direction: realising I wanted to make
something reflective. It fits my everyday life closely, and it's something I
need myself. That moment was quite exciting, because I'd found what I actually
wanted to build.

Last night, while testing the site, I wrote down roughly what I had done that
day. Seeing it listed, I felt I'd actually done quite a lot, and that felt
good. It's the feeling I want the app to give people: when your mind says you
haven't done enough, the record says otherwise.

This week I rejected or corrected the agent's suggestions several times. It
suggested scoring your own attention, and I changed that to how you felt about
your effort, because a score feels like judgement. When I thought about making
the list of what you did public, it pointed out that this clashed with my own
rule, and I kept it private. The design skill first proposed a purple landing
page, which didn't fit at all. All of this showed me that the direction is
mine to control. The agent can build, but I decide what the app is.
