# Crit 10 reflection

The breakthrough this week was the idea of the little one, a companion that
keeps you company while you work. It makes the app much more appealing, and it
still says what the app is about. I wanted the app to be more fun to use.
People often start work meaning to rest after a while, then get absorbed and
forget. An alarm only goes off once, so I wanted a reminder that's always
there. Something you can see, all the time, works better.

When I checked it myself, I found the little one wasn't moving any blocks at
all. It was only sliding sideways. The agent doesn't seem able to catch this
kind of problem, because it can only look at screenshots, and a still picture
can't show whether something is actually being built.

Next I want to make the records more complete, with daily, weekly and monthly
summaries. I also want to redesign the little one and the UI of the whole
site.
