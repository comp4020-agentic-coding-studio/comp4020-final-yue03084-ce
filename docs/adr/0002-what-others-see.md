# ADR 0002: What other people see live

Date: 2026-10-13. Status: accepted.

## Context

Crit 9 makes the app real-time: a change one person makes appears on everyone
else's page within about a second. Whatever is pushed out, everyone online sees
at once. So I had to decide what gets pushed to other people.

## Options

- **A. Make everything public.** The description, time spent, how it felt and
  the record of checks all go to everyone. Putting numbers on a public
  platform could lead to competition.
- **B. Make only the stopping public, and let each person choose whether to
  share.** Others see a one-line description and replies. Effort data is
  visible only to the person themselves.
- **C. Make nothing public.** If others can't see anything, the encouragement
  between users feels empty, just a light line of words.

## Decision

B. Some people want others to see what they did and to get a few replies.
Others want the whole experience to be private and don't like anything public.
Letting each person choose works for both. And unlike C, a shared "enough"
carries a concrete description of what was done. Even if someone doesn't know
your field, they can judge roughly how it went by common sense. With nothing at
all to go on, encouragement would be strange.

## Consequences

- Some people may never share, and the platform could feel quiet.
- How people describe their own effort, and how they feel seeing other
  people's, varies a lot. It's very subjective, so for now I can only judge it
  by my own feelings. At the crit, while my pod uses it, I'll ask what they
  think.
- People may feel this is only fake encouragement. And once you see it as
  nothing more than encouragement, it may stop working.
